Blog Reflection

Starting college is really great and exciting, but at the same time it scares me a bit. First of all, it is necessary to understand that getting higher education means responsibility for many things. There are such things as studying, working, extra-curriculum activities, social life, taking care of oneself and relaxing. There often comes a feeling that a student simply does not have enough time for everything. Management of time is one of the most difficult things to do for any student in college. First of all, it is necessary to understand that a student does not only study but also works and takes part in extra-curriculum activities. It is quite difficult to organize all this, as there is a necessity to consider everything. A student has to prepare an assignment for tomorrow but also he or she should work or attend some activity after working. Another element of college that creates problems for students is keeping themselves under a busy schedule. Students need to eat properly, have some physical activity, get enough rest, and take care of their psychological state. However, if a student has a busy schedule, it means that he will not be able to perform these actions. He does not eat regularly, works at night, does not do any sport anymore because he believes he needs to do a lot of things. Social life is one more thing that creates difficulty in coping with all aspects of the studying process. College is supposed to be the period of making friends and memorable moments. However, students do not want to spend their days only studying, but spending time partying makes the process of studying even harder. All of the aforementioned factors affect negatively both psychological and physical well-being of the students. Stress and delayed assignments make it hard for students to study. Besides, lack of sleep and unhealthy eating habits cause physical exhaustion. As I have understood from my experience, balance doesn’t have to be perfect in all aspects of life. College students need to know about their strong and weak sides and be able to recognize when to reduce the pace of work. It is necessary to attend college, have part-time jobs, socialize and look after themselves. However, it is equally important to have enough time for all of these activities.
